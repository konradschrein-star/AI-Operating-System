> **S3** — Longest stall attributable to **numbering**: max minutes a `pending`
> task spent with all of its `depends_on` `done` **and its workstream idle**.

### 5.1 The mechanism, cited by symbol

`spawnTaskRuns()` in `forge-control/src/lib/project-tick.ts` computes
`busyWorkstreams()` over the project's rows and then defers, via
`partitionByWorkstream()`, every claimed task whose `(project_id, workstream)`
key already holds a runner. Its own doc-comment states the invariant in its
first line — **"AT MOST ONE RUNNING TASK PER (project, workstream) — the
operator's ruling of round 222"** — and is explicit that the belt consults no
write-sets at all.

`b7ab4c57` ran **35 of 35 tasks in a single workstream, `main`**. One
workstream under a one-runner-per-workstream rule is a hard concurrency ceiling
of exactly 1, which is precisely what §3.1 measured. **The engine did not
malfunction. It did exactly what it is specified to do, and what it is specified
to do yields a peak of 1 when a planner opens one workstream.**

Cited by requirement rather than by line: this is the belt R16/R17 do *not*
control. `conflicts()` / `selectClaimable()` in `lib/task-graph.ts` decide
contention *within* a workstream and would have run these tasks together — R17
states an empty or disjoint write-set "is always claimable" — and then the
spawn-time belt holds the second one anyway.
